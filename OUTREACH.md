# WorkForce.dz — Outreach kit

Ready-to-send cold emails for airlines/airports, hotels, transport & fleet, and
warehouse/e-commerce operators. Copy, fill the `[brackets]`, send.

## How to use these (read once)

- **Keep it short.** Decision-makers skim. Every template below is under ~130 words on purpose.
- **Personalise the first line.** One real detail about *their* operation (a recent delay day, a new route, a seasonal peak) beats any clever pitch. Templates leave a `[specific detail]` slot for exactly this.
- **The ask is tiny.** You are not selling software in a cold email. You are asking for a 15-minute call or permission to send a 2-minute demo link. Low friction = more replies.
- **Be honest.** Never claim signed customers, real savings, or a live deployment you don't have. The saving figures are from simulation; you offer to **prove them on their data in a free pilot**. This honesty is a feature — say it.
- **Send in French to Algerian companies** unless you know they prefer English. French versions are below each English one.
- **Attach** the one-page value sheet (end of this file) as a PDF, and include your **demo link** + **website link**.
- **Follow up once** after 4–5 days (template at the end). Most replies come from the follow-up.

Placeholders to replace everywhere: `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `[Your name]`, `[demo link]`, `[site link]`, `[specific detail]`, `[phone]`.
