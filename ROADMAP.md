# What's next for Tawzia

Where the product stands, what it still lacks, and the order to fix that in.
Written October 2026.

## Where it stands

Tawzia is a working prototype, not an idea, built by one founder. It runs today on a fictional
140-person company on two sites (Algiers and Ouargla).

- **Nine agents work end to end.** Attendance, payroll, filings, compliance,
  retention, recruiting and the employee desk feed the approval inbox.
  Scheduling and onboarding run on their own screens and send their results
  to the inbox. Nothing is sent, filed, paid or rejected without a human click.
- **The rules are real.** The 2026 SNMG, CNAS and IRG rules live in one dated
  file and are tested against published examples. The labour-law checks cover
  night work, under-19s, the overtime cap and weekly rest.
- **Privacy is built in.** Sensitive fields are encrypted, personal data is
  masked before any AI call, a local model can keep everything on site, and
  every action is logged (Law 25-11).
- **AI assistants can reach it.** 28 MCP tools (10 public, 18 company).
- **What is missing is proof from real companies.** No company has used it on
  its own payroll yet, and the market and pricing numbers are still guesses.

The feedback "a good idea for an ideathon" points at exactly that gap: the
jury saw an idea because there is no customer yet. The next six months are
about closing it.

## Phase 1: earn the first users (months 0 to 3)

The goal is five companies running one real payroll month through Tawzia
alongside their current method, and comparing the two.

1. **Get the rules signed off.** Find one payroll accountant (cabinet
   comptable) to check the payroll engine against three real anonymised
   payslips, and to confirm the values still marked [verify]: the CNAS
   employer rate, the G50 late-filing surcharge, and the ANPDP breach
   deadline. A rules file stamped by a professional is the strongest trust
   signal available.
2. **Recruit pilots through accountants, not companies.** One accountant can
   bring several client firms. Offer them the multi-company console free
   during the pilot.
3. **Make the "parallel run" offer.** "Run October payroll in Tawzia next to
   your usual method. If the numbers differ, I find out why with you." It
   costs the company nothing, and every mismatch improves the engine.
4. **Rerun the HR interviews with sharper questions.** Earlier conversations
   gave little. Ask about events, not opinions: "Walk me through last month's
   G50: who did it, how long it took, and what went wrong." Count hours and
   errors.
5. **Measure three numbers per pilot:** hours spent on payroll and filings
   before and after, errors caught before filing, and days each filing went
   in early or late. These replace every assumption in the pitch.

6. **Find a second pair of hands.** Tawzia is a solo project. The first
   addition is a payroll or labour-law adviser, then a co-founder who can sell
   to accountants.

## Phase 2: make it deployable (months 3 to 6)

Phase 2 covers what a real company will ask for before it trusts Tawzia with its
payroll.

- **Real accounts and roles.** The company MCP server already checks tokens;
  connect a real sign-in provider so each HR manager, payroll officer and
  employee has their own login.
- **Hosting in Algeria.** Data stays in the country, with backups. This
  answers the Law 25-11 question before anyone asks it.
- **Import from what companies already use:** Excel templates first, then the
  common local payroll software exports.
- **WhatsApp for the employee desk.** Employees already live on WhatsApp. The
  desk logic is built; it needs the channel.
- **Verified documents.** Every certificate gets a QR code that a bank,
  landlord or embassy can scan to check it is genuine. It is useful on its
  own, and every scan advertises Tawzia.
- **The January update drill.** The 2027 Finance Law will change the rules.
  Ship it as a dated config update with an inbox card ("IRG rules updated,
  check your January payroll"). This proves the "the law changes, we absorb
  it" promise.

## Phase 3: grow (months 6 to 12)

- **Free public calculators:** net salary, employer cost, "is this shift
  legal?" and a monthly filing calendar. People search for these every
  January and before every 20th; each one ends with "let the agents do this
  for your whole company".
- **The accountant console as a product:** many client companies, one inbox,
  white-label payslips.
- **Connections to official portals, where they open up.** Today Tawzia
  prepares drafts and links to the CNAS and Jibaya'tic portals. Direct
  submission waits for official access and stays behind a human click.
- **Funding.** With pilots and measured savings, apply to the Algerian
  Startup Fund and the Startup Label (4 years of tax exemption), and to
  incubators.

## What Tawzia will not do yet

- File anything automatically with CNAS, DGI or ANEM.
- Make claims about savings or market size before pilots measure them.
- Expand outside Algeria. The Hijri-calendar engine travels to other markets,
  but the labour law does not.

## The one-line version

Tawzia is built. Now it needs five real payrolls, one accountant's signature
on the rules, and three numbers that prove the hours saved.
